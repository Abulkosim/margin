# Five-user follow-up 👤

Roadmap item 7.4. Get five people who use Claude Code or Codex, at least two on Windows. Ask about what they did, not what they think they might do. Never ask for tokens, emails, or screenshots with account details.

## Install

Watch if you can, or ask right after.

- OS and version, and which file they downloaded.
- Did they get past the Mac or Windows warning without help? If not, where did they stop?
- Minutes until the first real reading.
- How many accounts did Margin find, and how many do they have?

## Day 7

- Is Margin still running? If not, when did it stop and why?
- Did it come back after a reboot?
- Did an alert arrive? Was it useful, late, or noise?
- When did they last open the popover, and what were they checking?
- Have they told anyone about it?
