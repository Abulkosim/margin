# v0.6.0

2026-10-25 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/margin/releases/tag/v0.6.0)

- Accounts are ordered by usage remaining, lowest first, with the pinned account on top and signed-out accounts last. The order updates when you open Margin, not while you look. Change it in Settings → Appearance → Account order.
- Cards show the account's email next to the plan. Signed-out accounts show the exact sign-in command as a button that copies it.
- Opening Margin after a minute or more returns to the overview at the top.
- ⌘1 and ⌘2 switch between cards and compact (Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 on Windows). In compact, the limit that runs out first is shown in full ink, and the reset time belongs to it. ↑ and ↓ move between rows.
- "2 accounts need attention" is a button that jumps to the first one.
- Alerts and their settings use usage remaining, like the rest of the app: "Personal · Session 20% left", with the reset time in the body. The tray tooltip shows when the limit it follows resets.
- New installs follow the account with the lowest remaining in the tray and start in compact when three or more accounts are found.
- Margin does less work in the background. Opening it only refreshes readings older than 30 seconds, unchanged readings are stored once, and history files are about a third of their former size.
- Fixed an account getting stuck on "Checking usage" after Find accounts or a Keychain link changed its source.
