# v0.8.1

2026-10-27 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/margin/releases/tag/v0.8.1)

- Notifications, the tray tooltip and `margin --line --short` follow your system's 12- or 24-hour clock, like the popover.
- Make active names accounts by label and plan when two accounts share an email, for example "Claude Team (Team)" and "Personal (Max 20×)".
- The Add account hint tells you how to sign in first: on macOS `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=<folder> claude`, since the sign-in lives in the Keychain, and `CODEX_HOME=<folder> codex login` for Codex.
- Updated dependencies, including Tauri 2.12.5, Vite 8 and Playwright 1.64.
