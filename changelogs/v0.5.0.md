# v0.5.0

Unreleased

- The tray icon follows the session and weekly limits by default, skipping model-specific and expired limits. Change it in Settings → Tray shows.
- The tray icon is easier to read: a thicker ring, a filled centre dot at 20% or less remaining, and on Windows amber and red tiers that match a light or dark taskbar. On macOS, Settings → Show percentage next to icon adds the number beside it.
- Session and weekly limits show a pace line based on recorded history: "On pace", or the time usage runs out at the current rate. It appears after 30 minutes and 3 readings in the current period.
- Add account has a Choose folder… button, and removed accounts stay removed when you run Find accounts. Settings → Accounts → Show them brings them back.
- Model limits such as Fable, Opus and Sonnet show as chips under the weekly limit with the percentage left; hover or focus one for its reset time. Readings above 100% count as 100% instead of failing the refresh.
- Usage alerts are on for new installs. Choose thresholds from 50 to 100% in Settings → Notifications, and optionally get an alert when a limit resets. A limit already past a threshold when Margin first sees it alerts once. Clicking an alert opens Margin.
- Each limit shows the reset time next to the countdown, for example "Resets in 2h 18m · 16:30". Resets on another day add the weekday or date. Narrow windows show the countdown only.
- Account cards list available limit resets, such as "1 reset available · expires in 3d", for Claude and Codex. Margin only reads them; use them from Claude or Codex.
- Margin checks for updates once a day and puts a dot on Settings when one is available. It never installs on its own. Turn it off in Settings → Updates → Check for updates daily.
